# Heliocide Observatory Canon Lock

- The Blood Eclipse War lasted 170 years between the Drakken Empire under Mother's planetary will and the Administration.
- At Year 170, during the collapse at the Aureal Gate, the Drakken weaponized Starsilk for the first time. This was the first proof that the Shard God could be hurt.
- The Shard God's response is immediate, cold, strategic, deliberate containment and self-preservation. It is not panic, berserker rage, or simple retaliation.
- The first heliocide strike targets the Hal'Ven Cluster, forcing sequential stellar collapses and nascent singularities until the Siege Wall forms. Thousands of stars and trillions of lives are sacrificed.
- Starsilk is literal programmable cosmological substance. Starsilk Macros are literal repeatable action-loops embedded into reality.
- Physical Siege Wall presentation is a vast irregular swath of starless absence. It is never a visible lattice, grid, fence, wireframe, connector web, or network diagram. Tactical schematics may abstract containment geometry but must remain visibly identified as schematics.
- Death is final. No ghosts, trapped conscious souls, or ongoing conscious afterlife inside stars.
- There are no clean heroes. The observer cannot stop the heliocide.
- Current identity naming is exclusively **Shard God**.
